Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I try before joining?

Yes, your first session is free, including a class. Bring ID and trainers; we handle the rest.

Is it really open 24/7?

Yes for all-access members. Staffed 6am–11pm; outside those hours access is by member app with security monitoring.

Do you have ladies-only hours?

Ladies-only floor hours are 9am–12pm daily, plus female coaches on request for PT.

What about parking?

180 covered bays in Bay Square with 2 free hours validated for members.
