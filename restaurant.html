Our Story

Fifteen years of fire-cooked Levantine cooking

Chef Karim Haddad opened Bab Al Bahr in 2011 with one wood-fired oven and a family book of recipes from Beirut and Aleppo. Today the kitchen runs twelve stations, but the rule is the same: charcoal, olive oil from the Akkar valley, and nothing out of a freezer.

The dining room seats 90 inside and 40 on the terrace overlooking Dubai Marina Walk. Live oud on Thursday and Friday evenings.
