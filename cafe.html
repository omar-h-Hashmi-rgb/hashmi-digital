Questions, answered

Everything you usually ask before booking

Do you take bookings?

Tables of two walk in freely. Tables of five or more, and the private back room, are bookable by phone or WhatsApp.

Do you serve food?

Yes, an all-day brunch menu until 4 PM plus pastry from our own oven all day.

Is there parking?

Boulevard Point valet validates 2 hours with any order; overflow parking is 3 minutes away on foot.

Can I work from the café?

Yes. Fast Wi-Fi and plug points at most tables until 5 PM.

Do you sell beans?

Every roaster on the shelf is sold as 250g bags, ground to order for your brewer.
