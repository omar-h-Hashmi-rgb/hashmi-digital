Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We work with Daman, AXA, Cigna, MetLife and 20+ other insurers. Send us your card on WhatsApp and we will confirm your coverage before you come in.

Is the consultation really AED 250 with the scan included?

Yes, that is the complete first-visit price including 3D scan, X-rays and a written plan. No add-ons at the desk.

How fast can I be seen for an emergency?

We hold same-day emergency slots every morning. Message us before 10am and you will be seen the same day.

Do you offer instalments?

0% instalments over 6 months through Tabby and Tamara on any treatment above AED 2,000.
