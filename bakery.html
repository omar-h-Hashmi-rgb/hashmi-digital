Questions, answered

Everything you usually ask before booking

Can I get a cake today?

Same-day cakes happen when the kitchen allows it, call us first. 24 hours is the safe lead time.

Do you deliver?

Same-morning delivery in Jumeirah and Al Wasl; courier across Dubai for orders above AED 150.

Is the sourdough gluten-free?

No, our breads are naturally leavened wheat. We stock one certified gluten-free loaf daily.

Can I order for a whole week?

Yes, the weekly bread plan delivers Monday to Friday with rotating loaves.

Do you take corporate orders?

Yes, invoiced weekly plans and event boxes for offices and hotels.
