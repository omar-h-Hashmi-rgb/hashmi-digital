Questions, answered

Do you use genuine parts?

Your choice: genuine dealer parts or certified OEM-equivalent at roughly 40% less. Both carry our 12-month warranty and we show you both prices first.

Is pickup and drop really free?

Yes, anywhere in Dubai. Sharjah and Abu Dhabi arranged at a small fee, quoted up front.

Can I watch the repair?

You get live photo updates on WhatsApp at every stage. If you want to visit, our Al Quoz bays have a customer viewing area.

How long does a major service take?

Most are done same day. Jobs needing parts are typically 24 hours; we tell you at the quote stage, not after.
