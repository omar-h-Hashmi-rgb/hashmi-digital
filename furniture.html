Questions, answered

How long does a custom piece take?

Three to six weeks depending on fabric availability; your quote confirms the exact date.

Can I see fabrics before ordering?

Yes, in the showroom or during the free home visit with the full swatch set.

Is delivery included?

White-glove delivery and assembly across the UAE is included on every order.

Do you work with offices and clinics?

Yes, commercial fit-outs with invoiced billing and overnight installation.

What is the warranty?

Two years on structure and joinery, one year on upholstery, in writing on every invoice.
